## Content guardrails

- Every conversation has a distinct situation, objective, factual detail, decision, and follow-up.
- Flexible answers are explicit authored/audited forms; exact values and semantic constraints remain conservative.
- Topic IDs, canonical English, Vietnamese source sentences, learning objectives, and full conversation sequences must be unique.
- D1–D4 are validated separately at 451, 467, 483, and 500 topics before the next set is accepted.
